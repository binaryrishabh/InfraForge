## Internal overload and intervention proof

[m2b-proofs.json](m2b-proofs.json) is regenerated by
`bun run debug:workload --write`. It combines measured local HEAVY mean demand with
**authored** 16-entry queue, 100,000-us caller timeout and optional pause from
200,000 to 600,000 us. It does not claim externally measured fault behavior.

| Experiment | Offered | Admitted | Caller complete | Rejected | Deadline exceeded | Station complete / cancelled |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| overload, detach | 80 | 50 | 6 | 30 | 44 | 50 / 0 |
| pause, detach | 80 | 33 | 6 | 47 | 27 | 33 / 0 |
| pause, cooperative cancellation | 80 | 80 | 6 | 0 | 74 | 6 / 74 |

At 500,000 us in the detach/pause experiment, 51 arrivals have been offered,
all callers have terminated, but 16 queued and one active paused invocation remain.
CPU core-time stays at 200,000 us during the pause. Service resumes and retained work
drains. This demonstrates why caller in-flight count cannot substitute for remote
work populations. Cooperative cancellation changes those populations through the
configured capability, without altering arrival rate or adding a multiplier.

## Performance and cancellation decision

The pre-change kernel probe retained 100,000 no-op deadlines, taking about 61 ms to
schedule and 163 ms to process on this host. This established an actual retention
case before adding indexed removal. It was not a complete simulator benchmark.

[m2b-performance.json](m2b-performance.json) separately records 100,000-request lazy
periodic and bounded-trace runs. Both execute 300,042 events, cancel 100,000 completed
caller deadlines, peak at four pending events and finish with zero pending events.
Only 100 raw results are retained; all 100,000 outcomes contribute to distributions,
with 99,900 explicit raw omissions. Trace generation/validation, model setup, event
execution, aggregation and serialization are timed separately. Measured heap usage
before/after is descriptive and GC-dependent, not a retained-heap proof.

One recorded run took about 120 ms for trace generation, 12/392 ms for lazy/trace
setup, 1,033/910 ms for event execution, 3.9/2.3 ms for evidence aggregation and less
than 0.5 ms for serialization. Output was about 75/71 KB. These are local Bun 1.3.14
timings, not universal performance guarantees; instrumentation/provenance content
can change timings/size. Indexed cancellation adds no tombstone collection policy.

## Self-review and scope

Proven weaknesses fixed during self-review included observation-dependent floating
point service accounting (measurements now only project), oversized trace copying
before validation, recursive identity escaping on deep dependency paths, a precision
boundary in log2(MAX_SAFE_INTEGER), unqualified
arrival-rate applicability of local profiles, and a zero CPU counter becoming an
Infinity/null comparison. No new public physics, migration, challenge definition,
provider catalog, auth flow, UI styling or runtime packaging change was introduced.

Final validation uses frozen install, workspace/source/test type checking, lint and
boundaries, all workspace and tooling tests, reality/calibration artifact checks,
all 30 unchanged goldens, all ten unchanged challenge hashes, frontend build,
dependency audit and the existing disposable integration CI. No separate local
release rehearsal is needed for excluded internal code; the repository's existing
automatic CI packaging job remains unchanged. Final exact-SHA CI is reported with
the published commit, not inferred from a previous green revision.

Remaining limits: reserved-share CPU scheduling; one serial synchronous dependency;
caller-owned deadline only; instantaneous ideal cooperative cancellation; no retry
engine, service variability fit, transport, working-set memory, I/O devices, DB locks,
cache behavior or cloud-valid application profiles. The calibration candidate has
visible latency discrepancy and no external validity certification.

Recommended M2C: deepen compute scheduling, worker/CPU contention and memory working
sets around these workload/profile/lifecycle contracts; measure workload-specific
multi-worker behavior and service variation, preserving Unknown outside supported
domains. Add protocol-owned dependency cancellation/timeouts and retry budgets only
when their mechanisms are modeled. Keep Model 2 internal and production PARKED.
